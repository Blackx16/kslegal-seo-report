# KS Legal & Associates

> KS Legal & Associates is a premier, full-service Indian law firm headquartered in Mumbai with branch presence across Ahmedabad, Delhi, Bangalore, and Chennai. The firm specializes in corporate & commercial transactions, litigation & dispute resolution, insolvency & bankruptcy (NCLT/IBC), real estate & infrastructure, banking & finance, and intellectual property rights.

## Core Practice Areas

- [Corporate & Commercial Law](https://www.kslegal.co.in/corporate-commercial-laws/): Advisory on cross-border joint ventures, corporate governance, commercial contracts, regulatory compliance, middle-market M&A, and SEBI regulations.
- [Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code (IBC / NCLT)](https://www.kslegal.co.in/insolvency-bankruptcy/): Representation of financial creditors, operational creditors, and corporate debtors before the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) and NCLAT.
- [Litigation & Dispute Resolution](https://www.kslegal.co.in/litigation-dispute-resolution/): Civil and commercial litigation before the Bombay High Court, Supreme Court of India, district courts, and domestic/international commercial arbitration.
- [Banking & Financial Laws](https://www.kslegal.co.in/best-banking-and-financial-law-firm-mumbai/): Debt recovery (DRT/DRAT), SARFAESI proceedings, project finance, loan restructuring, and banking documentation.
- [Mergers & Acquisitions (M&A)](https://www.kslegal.co.in/mergers-acquisitions/): Due diligence, share purchase agreements, scheme of arrangements, and regulatory clearances.
- [Real Estate & Infrastructure](https://www.kslegal.co.in/real-estate-infrastructure/): Title verification, RERA litigation, property development contracts, lease agreements, and infrastructure advisory.
- [Labour & Employment Law](https://www.kslegal.co.in/labour-employment/): Employment contracts, workplace compliance, POSH compliance, industrial disputes, and executive compensation.
- [Intellectual Property Rights](https://www.kslegal.co.in/intellectual-property-rights/): Trademark registration, copyright protection, patent advisory, and IP infringement litigation.
