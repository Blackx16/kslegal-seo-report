## Direction contract

**THESIS**: Refuses the generic category default of fragmented accordion folders and decorative scorecards that force users to click through 14 siloed sections to understand site health; replaces it with a unified dual-lens architecture uniting an executive strategic risk dossier with an actionable cross-category diagnostic triage matrix and copy-paste remediation drawers.

**OWN-WORLD**: High-density instrument telemetry aesthetic. Deep obsidian (#0a0f1d) and slate-glass (#131b2e) ground with razor 1px borders (#1e293b); calibrated status channels in glowing coral (#ef4444), radiant amber (#f59e0b), and emerald mint (#10b981). Monospace code terminals paired with clean, geometric system sans-serif typography.

**STORY**: In the first 10 seconds, leadership grasps overall health (46/100, Grade F) and the 3 business threats; developers switch to the Workstation, instantly filter all critical blockers across 14 categories, inspect exact diagnostic proof, and copy validated configuration fixes directly into production.

**FIRST VIEWPORT**: Sticky control header (domain pill, timestamp, dual-view toggle pill [Executive Briefing | Diagnostic Workstation], theme switch, PDF export) sitting above an arc gauge health telemetry bar; followed directly by the 3-column business threat cards (Executive view) or the live-searchable Cross-Category Triage Matrix with expandable code diff drawers (Workstation view).

**FORM**: Dual-Lens Diagnostic Workstation, combining executive risk projection with telemetry triage table; seed key `ae47a486`.

**FINISH**: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
